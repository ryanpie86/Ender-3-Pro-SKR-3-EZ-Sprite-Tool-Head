CR Touch cable, pin by pin: SKR Mini E3 V3 → SKR 3 EZ

The Sprite's probe cable ends in one 5-pin plug that sat in the Mini E3's Z-PROBE header. The SKR 3 EZ wants the same five crimps split over SERVOS (3-pin) and Probe (2-pin). Nothing is re-crimped; the crimps move housing to housing.

Which end of the black plug is pin 1

  1. Leave the cable plugged into the CR Touch. The two grounds are joined inside the probe.
  2. Meter on continuity: find the two crimps in the 5-pin housing that beep together. They are pins 2 and 5.
  3. The one of those two that sits at an end of the housing is pin 5 (servo ground). The opposite end is pin 1 (signal).
  4. If the beeping pair sits at positions 1 and 4 counted from your starting end, you started at pin 5: count from the other end.

Moving the crimps

  1. Lift each retaining tab on the 5-pin JST-XH housing with a pin or a fine blade and slide the crimp out, one at a time, keeping its number.
  2. Push crimps 5, 4, 3 into a 3-pin XH housing in that order (positions 1, 2, 3). Push crimps 2, 1 into a 2-pin XH housing (positions 1, 2). Each crimp's tab clicks under the housing window; one that will not click has its tab bent flat and needs lifting with a blade.
  3. Before power: continuity between SERVOS pin 1 and Probe pin 1 (both grounds), none between SERVOS pin 2 and either ground.
  4. Plug SERVOS in with its pin 1 at the left (nearest TFT), Probe with its pin 1 at the left (nearest SERVOS). The housings only fit one way round on the shrouded headers.

Proving it

  1. QUERY_PROBE → probe: open with nothing touching the pin.
  2. BLTOUCH_DEBUG COMMAND=pin_down drops the pin, COMMAND=pin_up lifts it.
  3. Hold the pin up with a finger: QUERY_PROBE → TRIGGERED.
  4. Always TRIGGERED, or a pin that never moves, means both housings are end-for-end: pull them and reverse both.
