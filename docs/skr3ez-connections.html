Jumpers and switches, final state

  1. USB / CAN switch next to the USB socket: popped up (USB).
  2. VUSB (under the VIN header): open. Close only for a bench flash without the PSU, and never together with 24 V.
  3. Motor-voltage 3-pin header beside each driver's capacitor, ×5: cap on the upper pair, nearest the motor socket (DCIN).
  4. DIAG 2-pin at each driver's upper-left, ×5: none fitted.
  5. V-FAN next to HE1: cap on VIN. VOT and VIN 2×4 headers empty.
  6. HE0 and HE1 output jumpers (PB3, PB4) beside the MOSFETs: both fitted.
  7. PT1000 jumpers at TH0 and TH1: both open (100 K NTC).
  8. EZ2209 ×5 in the red sockets at X, Y, Z, E0, E1. No mode jumpers exist on the EZ board.

Order of work

  1. With everything unplugged, meter the PSU leads: 24 V, + and − as marked at DCIN.
  2. Thermistors first (TH0, TB), then the USB to the Pi. Klipper should come up reading room temperature.
  3. Motors, endstops, CR Touch, runout sensor, fans.
  4. Heaters last, with the ferrules torqued and nothing stray in the terminal block.
  5. LCD ribbons. Then the commissioning list in docs/05.

Cable route in the enclosure: everything comes in over the rear wall notch; motors and endstops run down the rail-side channel, heater, bed and PSU leads down the screen-side channel to the terminals at the front. Only the USB plug leaves through the right wall.
